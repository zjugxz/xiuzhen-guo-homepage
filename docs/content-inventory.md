## Research

Introductory sentence: research focuses on wireless intelligence across four interconnected directions.

### Intelligent Wireless Systems

Combine machine learning with wireless communication and networking so wireless systems can predict channel conditions, adapt to changing environments, and provide reliable connectivity.

### Battery-Free And Ultra-Low-Power IoT

Develop backscatter communication, energy-efficient hardware, and networking technologies that allow IoT devices to communicate and operate with minimal or no battery power.

### Wireless And Wearable Sensing

Use radio signals, magnetic fields, acoustic signals, and emerging sensing modalities to understand human activities, monitor health conditions, and perceive the physical environment.

### Physical And Embodied Intelligence

Integrate AI models with wireless devices, programmable hardware, sensors, and robots so intelligent agents can perceive, communicate, and interact with the real world.
